The last transmission from the RMS Titanic, 
The Hindenburg Fire that sent everyone in panic,
The blinding light that tasted metallic,
The roll of the die they called satanic. 
There are many things thoughtful,
Others manic.

The wistful efforts that bled in vain, 
Surely somewhere someone’s pain.
When the shouting leaves a stain,
Some thoughts at first insane,
Soon take root and make their claim.

Somewhere sometime one of these days,
When someone dies another one pays. 
Who decides what should stay? 
Why decry when they shout nay?

The lives of the many outweigh the few.
Is that why the soldiers stew?
I looked at myself and always knew.
Oh how, how it blew.
