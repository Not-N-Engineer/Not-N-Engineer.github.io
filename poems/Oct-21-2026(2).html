Goodbye old lady of the wood.
Where you were, I misunderstood.

How are you now,


    Dear Lady of the Wood,

    Magnificence in breadth, magnificent in breath. 
I hope I find you well. 
    When you ran, I held you hand in hand.
I wish I held on longer. 
    In auburn evenings, we seldom left before the moon.
In starry night, we would part, and swear to see each other soon.
Then you and I would meet together again and dance until it was noon. 
I remember your favorite song was Claire de Lune in the dead of night.
    When I left I’m sure I gave you quite a fright.

    Sorry.
    Signed,
    The old lady of the wood
