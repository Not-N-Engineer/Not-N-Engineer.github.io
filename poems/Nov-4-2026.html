Mildew,
Dancing in the sunlight.

(I can never)
See you,
Even on the last night.

Prancing,
Ponies in blue sky.

(I can never)
Hear you,
Even if it’s goodbye.

Why can’t you see,
When you’ve got enough out of me,

Can’t stick around,
Even for a while,
Like I weigh a pound,
Drifting over smooth tile.

(I will always)
Love you,
Even if that’s your style.

Tree tops,
Glistening green out for miles.

(I will always)
Miss you,
Even though I can’t smile.
