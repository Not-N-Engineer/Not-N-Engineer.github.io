I wish I wasn't me

Sometimes I can forget that I’m me.
I find in that emptiness that I can be happy.

When at school, I can set it aside.
Sometimes. 
Then it springs back up and I try to wash it away. 

It may be a bad habit to spend hours on YouTube,
but if you felt that same feeling of calm,
like quiet after a rainstorm,
wouldn't you too?

Sometimes even that doesn't work. 
And I lie on the floor
in the cold and dark
and let it wash over me
and think desperately.
