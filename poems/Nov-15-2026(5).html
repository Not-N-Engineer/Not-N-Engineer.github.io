A time passed.
A life lived, half-assed.

Watching

The dust settle,
On all the things now marred.
The overgrown nettles,
Taking over the yard.
The dying flower petals,
Falling on a table sheened with lard.

Everything nice once was made.
What now is loved is but hidden in the haze. 

The time passed. 
Over a life spent at long last.

Watching

How the mold
Clinging to the ceiling grew. 
How the birds
Turning quickly rancid flew. 
How very old
Becomes all that once was new.

How everything good must fade.
How everything loved must be only in a daze.
