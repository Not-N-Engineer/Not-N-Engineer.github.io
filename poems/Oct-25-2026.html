Morning comes to sing a little song. 
Birds fly and land 
and the world 
does its little dance. 
I get the sense that I’ve been here
far too long. 

When afternoon comes and the shadows grow long,
bees buzz with activity
and we all
march along.
I get the sense that I’ve been here
far too long. 

As evening falls and everything stalls,
our drum majors proudly salute, 
as everyone deserving 
cheers for their win. 
I get the sense that I’ve been here
and done nothing at all. 

Night comes in a blanket of blue. 
Crickets chirp stably
and I sit here
where I was years ago
and where I’ll surely be
In a few years too. 

Four A.M. and music plays, but not a song. 
What couldn’t I have done
While imagining myself
as someone else. 
I get the sense that I’ve been here
far too long.
