Forever is a long time
   to spend wandering

                                               why

           in the corner of the dark room so cold,
imagining colors in pale and bold,

      the sky blue paint, dried,
chips off the old fence under orange skies.

                                    You wonder why.

           Dreaming of your dreary house now sold,
colors of rainbow seemingly stale and gold,

      old ambition comes far to die,
here where the air stands stagnant, stale,
                
                  reminiscing on American Pie. 

                                    You wonder why.
