Maybe one day life will be as you dreamed
When waking home alone in the rain. 

Maybe one day the matches will sell,
Will one day light the heat in your house.

Maybe one day you’ll walk home alone,
To find again the warmth of your spouse.

Maybe one night the lights will be bright,
And everything will be just alright. 

Maybe one day life will be as it seems,
And the sunlight will be as warm when it beams.

Maybe one day one day away,
Everything is going to be okay.
