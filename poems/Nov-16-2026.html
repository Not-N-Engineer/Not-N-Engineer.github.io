And Caesar’s standing now 

Where he once had stood,
Holding everything that once was good,
Daring everything that understood,

Come and take me here right now,
Come and take your final bow,
And I’ll beat you like a mighty sow!

That, he screamed in a mighty voice,
Almost like he’d had a choice, 
And all that could withstand rejoiced,

‘Cause Caesar had a mighty hand,
Had struck all the members of his band. 
It seemed like he’d already withstood
All the pressure that he could. 

And Caesar’s standing now

Eons past and time passed on,
With cries ringing and dancing pawns
And all that bade with dying fawns and

Come and try to take me down, 
And whence you try again you’ll frown, 
For from my mouth rings mighty sound,

And with that line he passed finally. 
And all that time he ran and swam and sanded steadily.
And after he thought he had exhausted all his capability, mental stability, faculties and sanity

Daring now are we,
For living out our legacy. 
Fading, fading out,
While burning like a water spout. 

The sky opened up with much disgrace,
So much he couldn’t even show his face,
Before it swirled down on the dot,
And killed him right there on the spot.
